Privacy Policy

I, Uzan Kataoka, run this website personally, to record and share my own activities and the classes I teach. It is not operated by Minato City or by any Minato City facility.

This website uses Google Analytics to measure the number of visitors and similar information. This policy therefore sets out how information I receive, and website usage data, are handled.

1. Information I receive and how it is used

Information you send me by email when applying for a lesson or making an enquiry is used solely to reply to your enquiry and to contact you about lesson schedules and arrangements. It is not used for any other purpose.

2. Disclosure to third parties

I do not disclose the information you send me to any third party without your consent, except where required by law.

3. Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to understand how the site is used and to improve its content.

Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as the pages you view, the date and time of your visit, how long you stay, the referring website, your approximate location, and the browser and device you use. This information is collected anonymously and does not include your name, contact details or any other information that identifies you personally.

The information collected is transmitted to and managed by Google in accordance with its privacy policy. For details of how Google uses this data, please see:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Privacy Policy

If you prefer not to allow cookies, you can disable them in your browser settings. You may also install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, which stops this measurement on every website you visit, including this one.

4. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised without prior notice as laws or services change. The revised version takes effect when it is published on this website.
